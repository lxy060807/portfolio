Baoshi Hill entrance routes:
Six routes are assembled from connected OpenStreetMap road and trail nodes. The way IDs, ranges and source links for each route are recorded in atlas-trails.js. Colours identify approaches and grey represents shared segments. Route lengths are the horizontal sum of displayed path segments. Elevation is draped over the Copernicus terrain displayed in this model.
Huanglong Cave front gate reference: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Entrance_of_Huanglong_Cave_in_Hangzhou,_202506.jpg
The front gate marker is separate from the rear trailhead. The missing in-park link is not drawn. The Hama Peak overlook marker represents a nearby mapped trail point.

Causeways and stone pagodas:
Su and Bai Causeway centre lines and bridge way references use OpenStreetMap. Way IDs are recorded in atlas-waterfront.js. The bridge cores, approaches, revetments and planting are simplified to fit the atlas; individual railings and trees are representative.
Su Causeway heritage record: https://z.hangzhou.com.cn/011xhsy/content/2011-06/21/content_3772923.htm
Bai Causeway and Broken Bridge: https://z.hangzhou.com.cn/011xhsy/content/2011-06/21/content_3773072.htm
Three Pools layout and stone pagoda survey: https://whc.unesco.org/uploads/nominations/1334.pdf#page=135 (Figs. 2a-92 and 2a-93). The survey documents the 62 m triangle, 0.92 m body diameter and 2.32 m height. The southern reference position comes from OpenStreetMap node 9025997767; the two remaining positions are reconstructed from the plan's north orientation and the documented spacing.

Modeling notes:
Local equirectangular projection centred at 120.142° E, 30.248° N. One browser scene unit equals 1 km. Blender export is converted to metres.
Sourced heights and floor counts are used where available. Floor counts use a 3 m per floor conversion, and missing building heights are represented at 5.5 m. Landmark towers and rocks use simplified geometry. Vegetation is illustrative.
The modeled extent is the lake and nearby neighborhoods within the dataset bounding box, not the entire administratively defined scenic area. The model is not for navigation or engineering measurement.
